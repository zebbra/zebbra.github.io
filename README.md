# zebbra.github.io

The landing page at the root of zebbra's GitHub. It exists because that URL used to be a 404.

A meadow of red grass moves in the wind, a zebra rises out of it and looks up, and four seconds later you are on your way to [zebbra.ch](https://zebbra.ch/). Clicking, typing or scrolling cancels the move, so anyone who wants to read the page can.

One file, no dependencies. Project pages such as [zebra-timer](https://zebbra.github.io/zebra-timer/) are unaffected — they live on their own paths.

The zebra, the grass blades and the logomark are the same artwork as in [zebbra/zebra-timer](https://github.com/zebbra/zebra-timer); the SVG is copied in rather than shared, so a change there does not reach here on its own.
